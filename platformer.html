WHAT WE ARE TESTING
Can a decision model learn when to jump?
The road asked for a lane. This game asks for one of six button combinations. First compare the original models with a visible scripted reference. Then collect trajectories and train a platformer-specific model.
Initial check: the scripted reference finishes the level. Clef’s first JSON run fell into the first gap; original Liquid initially chose to wait on JSON. These models are experimental here.
Your road fine-tunes are still in the Road lab. They predict left / middle / right; they have not been trained to jump. Liquid here is the original 600M model, not those fine-tuned weights.
What exists already?
typesafe-mario ↗ and mario-jev ↗ are community Jev controllers for NES Mario. They read emulator state, rather than screenshots. We have not confirmed a TypeSafe-authored Mario implementation.
SuperTux ↗ is a full open-source desktop platformer. This browser experiment has its own art, physics and single level; it is not the complete Nintendo game.
Hosted calls go directly to OpenRouter. Local Liquid downloads weights from Hugging Face and runs on your device. Each attempt stops after 200 decisions; exporting keeps the trace without your key.
